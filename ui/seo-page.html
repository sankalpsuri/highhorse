Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need SEO if I already run Google Ads?
Yes — Google Ads gets you visibility while campaigns are active, but SEO builds long-term, compounding organic traffic that keeps generating leads even when you pause paid spend. The two work best together.
How do you choose keywords for my business?
We start with search-demand and competitor research to find high-intent keywords your customers actually use, then map them to the pages best positioned to rank and convert for each one.
Do backlinks still matter for SEO?
Yes. Backlinks remain one of the strongest trust signals search engines use to rank sites — we focus on earning high-quality, relevant links rather than volume.
Do you optimize websites for AI search and generative results?
Yes — alongside traditional SEO, we structure content and authority signals so your brand is more likely to be surfaced in AI-generated answers and featured snippets.
